# Content Style

Primary language is English. Do not translate the main product into Chinese in v1.

Tone:

- Direct
- Useful
- Skeptical of fake discounts
- Builder-focused

Every deal should answer:

- What is the actual offer?
- Who is eligible?
- What is the catch?
- When does it expire?
- Is the source official?
- Is there an affiliate relationship?

Avoid:

- "Guaranteed"
- "Risk-free"
- "Must buy"
- "Free money"
- Investment language for token or airdrop campaigns
- Unverified historical-low claims
