# Affiliate Application Kit

Use this copy when applying to partner programs. Keep claims conservative until traffic is measurable.

## Site description

Builder Deals Intel is an English deal intelligence site for builders, founders, engineers, and operators who buy AI tools, SaaS products, hosting, cloud credits, API platforms, and developer infrastructure.

The site does not publish blind coupon spam. Every listing includes a source URL, deal URL, confidence score, risk tags, region or eligibility notes, and affiliate disclosure when relevant.

## Audience

- Startup founders and indie builders validating tools before buying.
- Engineers looking for cloud/API credits and developer tool discounts.
- Operators comparing SaaS, newsletter, automation, hosting, and AI workflow products.

## Promotion methods

- SEO deal pages and category pages.
- Daily Top 10 builder deals newsletter.
- Source-labeled product deal listings.
- Sponsored placements clearly separated from editorial picks.
- Direct outreach to tool buyers on builder communities after content quality is established.

## Disclosure policy

Affiliate and sponsored placements are labeled. Paid partnerships do not bypass source verification, risk labels, eligibility notes, or editorial review.

## Payout information needed from owner

Pause applications when a platform asks for:

- PayPal, Wise, Payoneer, Stripe, or bank payout account.
- Tax form, business registration, or personal identity verification.
- Phone/email verification controlled by the owner.
